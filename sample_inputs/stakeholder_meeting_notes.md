# Stakeholder Meeting Notes

## Project

Customer Support Request Management Platform

## Current Process

Customer support requests are received through email, phone calls, and spreadsheets.

Support agents manually record requests and update request status in separate files.

Managers do not have a centralized view of open, pending, escalated, or resolved requests.

Customers frequently contact the support team multiple times because they do not receive automatic status updates.

## Business Expectations

The business wants a centralized platform where customers can submit support requests.

Support agents should be able to review, update, and resolve assigned requests.

Managers should be able to:

- Assign requests to support agents
- Monitor team workload
- Track resolution time
- Identify overdue requests
- Review escalated cases
- Analyze service performance

The platform should send notifications whenever the request status changes.

Only authorized employees should be able to access customer information.

The system should maintain a complete audit history of request updates.

## Stakeholders

- Customers
- Support Agents
- Support Managers
- Operations Leadership
- IT Team
- Compliance Team

## Open Questions

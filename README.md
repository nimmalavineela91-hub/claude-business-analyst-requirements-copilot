# Claude-Powered Business Analyst Requirements Copilot

## Project Overview

This portfolio project demonstrates how Claude can support Business Analysts by transforming stakeholder notes, business problem statements, and policy documents into structured requirements artifacts.

The solution is designed as an AI-assisted requirements copilot, where Claude generates draft outputs and a Business Analyst reviews, refines, and approves the final deliverables.

## Business Problem

Business Analysts often spend significant time reviewing meeting notes, identifying requirements, documenting user stories, defining acceptance criteria, and preparing UAT scenarios.

Important details may also be missed when stakeholder inputs are incomplete, ambiguous, duplicated, or conflicting.

This project uses Claude to support the requirements lifecycle while maintaining human review, traceability, and responsible AI controls.

## Core Capabilities

- Analyze stakeholder meeting notes
- Identify business problems, goals, and stakeholders
- Extract business and functional requirements
- Generate user stories and acceptance criteria
- Detect ambiguous, duplicate, and conflicting requirements
- Identify assumptions, dependencies, risks, and open questions
- Generate UAT scenarios
- Create executive summaries
- Maintain human review and approval

## Planned Workflow

Stakeholder Notes or Business Documents  
→ Claude Analysis  
→ Draft Requirements  
→ User Stories and Acceptance Criteria  
→ Risks, Assumptions, and Open Questions  
→ Business Analyst Review  
→ Approved BRD, FRD, and UAT Package

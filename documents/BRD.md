# Business Requirements Document

## Project Name
Claude-Powered Business Analyst Requirements Copilot

## Business Problem
Business Analysts spend significant time reviewing stakeholder notes and manually creating structured requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, risks, assumptions, and clarification questions.

## Proposed Solution
Develop an AI-assisted requirements copilot using Anthropic Claude. The solution will transform unstructured stakeholder inputs into draft business analysis artifacts that can be reviewed and approved by a Business Analyst.

## Target Users
- Business Analysts
- Product Owners
- Project Managers
- QA Analysts
- Scrum Masters

## Key Features
- Stakeholder notes analysis
- Functional requirement generation
- Non-functional requirement generation
- User story creation
- Acceptance criteria generation
- Risk and assumption identification
- Stakeholder clarification questions
- Human review and approval

## Expected Business Benefits
- Reduce manual documentation effort
- Improve requirement consistency
- Identify missing or ambiguous information
- Accelerate Agile backlog preparation
- Maintain human review and governance
